## 5. REVENUE SHARE AND PAYMENTS

5.1. **Revenue Share**: Publisher pays Licensor **80% of Net Revenue** received from the Sublicensed Platform.

5.2. **Reporting**: Share calculated solely on Platform reports, final and binding. No obligation to pay unless amounts actually received.

5.3. **Accrual**: Subject to actual receipt, revenue share credited to MC Pay account **within 60 days after end of reporting month**.

5.4. **Currency**: USD.

5.5. **Settlement**: Credited to MC Pay account. Licensor must register MC Pay. Withdrawal paid **within 10 banking days** after clicking "Withdraw".

5.6. **Fees/Taxes**: Transfer/bank/conversion/processing fees deducted from remuneration. Licensor bears own taxes.

## 8. TERM, TERMINATION

8.1. **Term**: Effective on Effective Date, indefinite.

8.2. **Game License Term (per-game)**: For each Game, 12-month fixed initial term from acceptance. Automatically renews for successive 12-month periods (Renewal Term) unless terminated per §8.3. Each Game independent.

8.3. **Renewal/Non-Renewal**: Either Party may elect not to renew by **60 days' prior written notice** before expiry. Otherwise auto-renews 12 months.

8.4. **Withdrawal by Licensor**: **Licensor cannot withdraw/revoke/remove a Game during the Initial 12-month term.** After expiry, may request removal with **60 days' notice** specifying the Game. Effective upon expiry of notice period; Game may stay live during notice + wind-down up to 30 days.

8.5. **Termination for Cause**: Either may terminate immediately on material breach unremedied within 10 days. On termination: all licenses end; Publisher stops exploitation best-efforts; accrued rights/payment obligations survive.

8.5A. **Termination for Convenience**: **Publisher may terminate for convenience on 15 days' notice. Licensor may terminate for convenience on 60 days' notice.** Doesn't affect Game License Terms in force (continue until scheduled expiry, no auto-renew).

8.6. **Survival**: Articles 6 (Warranties/Liability) and 7 (Confidentiality) + accrued payment obligations survive.
